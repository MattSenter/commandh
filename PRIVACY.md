# CommandH Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

CommandH collects no data. None.

- It makes no network connections. The App Store version runs in the App Sandbox without network access.
- It has no account, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
- It does not read your screen, your windows' contents, or your keystrokes, and it asks for no Accessibility, Screen Recording, or Input Monitoring permission.
- It looks only at which apps are running and whether each is hidden, on your computer, to decide when Command-H needs help. That information never leaves your computer and is not stored.
- Its three settings (Enabled, Show in menu bar, Open at login) are saved on your computer only.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MattSenter/commandh/issues.
